Safeguards that travel with the scorecard

Non-negotiable decision boundaries.
In force
  • ✓Do not convert workload FTE-equivalents into people counts.
  • ✓Missing evidence means unknown, never “no capability”.
  • ✓No model-based surplus labels or automated placement decisions.
  • ✓Use confirmed destination demand before any placement offer.
  • ✓Payroll data remains outside the assessment model.
Day 90 is an operating pilot proof point, not completed redeployment or final benefits.

Scope and what this proof cannot establish

The 90-day proof is intentionally concentrated.
~11.5% deep scope

Metering & Manual Operations represents ~6,000 of 52,000 employees. The remaining ~46,000 receive high-level exposure triage and the architecture framework, not individual capability assessment or intervention in this window. A successful Metering pathway is a proof point to inform the next phase; it does not establish that the same pathway works across other families.

This is a decision boundary

4,200 FTE-equivalents represent estimated workload change—not a count of employees to exit. The next decision is to validate tasks and receiving-unit demand, then invite a small cohort to evidence-based assessment. No one is labelled surplus by this view.
